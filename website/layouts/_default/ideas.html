How to choose

Coverage checklist

  • Does it make QRL 2.0 easier to use, build on, monitor, or explain?
  • Does it fill a child category with few or no current listings?
  • Does its project type describe what it is and its category describe its capability?
  • Can it be maintained by a small team or community contributor?
  • Can the project publish enough information for others to evaluate and list it?
